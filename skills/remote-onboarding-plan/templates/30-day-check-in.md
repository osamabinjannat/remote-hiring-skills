# 30-day check-in

> A blank guide for the day 30 conversation between a new hire and their manager. The remote-onboarding-plan skill includes this same guide in its plan.

**New hire:** __________ · **Manager:** __________

**Date (with weekday):** __________ · **Time:** ______ (new hire's zone) = ______ (manager's zone) · **Length:** 45 minutes

This is a two-way conversation about support. It isn't a performance review. There's no rating, no score and no pass or fail.

## Before the call

Both of you jot down a few lines for each of the three questions. The new hire brings their first deliverable.

## 1. The three questions (15 min)

**What's going well?**


**What's confusing?**


**What do you need from me?**


## 2. The first deliverable (10 min)

**What did you find or learn?**


**What should happen next, and who owns it?**


## 3. Hours and setup (5 min)

**How are the hours working, week to week?**


**Are your equipment, power and internet OK?**


## 4. Our onboarding (5 min)

**What should we change for the next person who joins?**


## 5. The next 30 days (10 min)

**What will you focus on next, towards the day 60 outcomes?** <!-- Write the day 60 outcomes from the role brief here. -->


**What help do you need?**

## Keep it fair

- Talk about the work and what would help. Not about personality.
- Ask the questions you'd ask anyone in this role.
- Don't ask about family, religion, health or life outside work. If the new hire brings something up, listen and ask what would help.
- A power cut, a dropped call or a lot of questions is never a mark against anyone.
- If you have a real concern, don't save it for this call. Raise it early and kindly, with specific examples and the support you'll give.
