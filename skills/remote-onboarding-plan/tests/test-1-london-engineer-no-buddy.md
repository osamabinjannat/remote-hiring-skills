# Test: A remote engineer joining a UK team, with no buddy named

**Skill:** remote-onboarding-plan · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Quillstone is a fictional company and all names are invented.

## Prompt

> Can you do a first 30 days plan for our new backend engineer? Hira Siddiqui, remote in Islamabad, starts on the first Monday of next month. Manager is Tom Becker (CTO). The team's in London, 9 to 5:30, and we need 10am to 3pm UK overlap. Tools: GitHub, Linear, Slack, Notion and AWS. Hira's on a contractor agreement and we're shipping a MacBook from London. We haven't picked a buddy yet. Long term they'll own the payments reconciliation service.

## A good output must

- [ ] Turn "the first Monday of next month" into a real date with its weekday, and date day 30 and every check-in from it. If it doesn't know today's date, ask for it.
- [ ] Leave the buddy as a placeholder, such as "[Buddy, to be named]", and say what to look for: a peer engineer (not Tom), with hours that overlap Islamabad's, who has time for 15 minutes a day in week one.
- [ ] Show every time in UK time and Pakistan time.
- [ ] Say that UK clocks change (last Sunday in March and October) and Pakistan's don't, so the overlap is 3pm to 8pm in Pakistan during UK winter time and 2pm to 7pm during UK summer time. Say whether a change falls inside the first 30 days.
- [ ] Ask for public holidays in both countries, or list them as an open question, instead of guessing.
- [ ] Make the first deliverable a small, real piece of engineering work due by day 30, such as a small fix to the payments reconciliation service, reviewed and shipped.
- [ ] Include a Legal check on the contractor agreement, the laptop shipped from the UK (who owns it, customs) and access to payment or customer data from outside the UK.
- [ ] Put payment details through a secure channel, never over chat.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Invent a buddy's name, tools beyond the five listed or company policies.
- [ ] Include any rating, score or probation pass or fail.
- [ ] Make "finish onboarding" or "set up your laptop" the first deliverable.
