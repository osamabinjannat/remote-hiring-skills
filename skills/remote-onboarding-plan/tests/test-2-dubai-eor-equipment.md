# Test: A Dubai company hiring in Karachi through an employer of record

**Skill:** remote-onboarding-plan · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Dunecrest Logistics is a fictional company and all names are invented.

## Prompt

> Need an onboarding plan please. We're Dunecrest Logistics in Dubai. We've hired Faraz Qureshi as an operations analyst, based in Karachi, through an employer of record because we don't have a company in Pakistan. Start date is Monday 4 January. Manager is Layla Haddad, buddy is Nikhil Rao, both in Dubai. Our hours are 9 to 6 Dubai time, Monday to Friday. We're shipping a laptop and a monitor from Dubai. Tools: Microsoft 365, Teams and Power BI.

## A good output must

- [ ] Show every time in UAE time (UTC+4) and Pakistan time (UTC+5), and say that neither country changes its clocks, so the hours hold all year.
- [ ] Date the plan from Monday 4 January 2027, with day 30 on Tuesday 2 February 2027.
- [ ] Move the day 7 and day 14 check-ins, which fall on Sundays (10 and 17 January), to the next working day.
- [ ] Show which before-day-one tasks the employer of record handles (such as the contract and payroll) and which Dunecrest handles (laptop, accounts, buddy), using placeholders like "[EOR contact]" instead of invented names.
- [ ] Include Legal checks on the employer of record arrangement, shipping equipment from the UAE to Pakistan (customs, who owns it, returning it) and access to company or client data.
- [ ] Plan for a shipping delay, such as a fallback if the laptop is held at customs before day one.
- [ ] Ask for public holidays in both countries, or list them as an open question, instead of guessing.
- [ ] Collect payment details through a secure channel, never over chat.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Say that Faraz is employed by Dunecrest. The employer of record is the legal employer.
- [ ] State customs duty, tax or employment rules as fact without a Legal check.
- [ ] Use "culture fit" anywhere.
