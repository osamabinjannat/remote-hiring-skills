# Debrief agenda: [Candidate name or ID], [Role title]

## Before you book it
- [ ] Every interviewer has sent written feedback. It's due within 24 hours of each interview. Don't book the debrief until it's all in.
- [ ] Each interviewer wrote their feedback alone, before reading anyone else's.
- [ ] The debrief is within 2 working days of the last interview.
- [ ] Everyone has the scorecard (the list of competencies, their weights and the rating scale).

## Ground rules (read these out)
1. Evidence first, ratings second. Say what the candidate said or did, then give your rating.
2. The most junior person speaks first. The hiring manager speaks last.
3. "Gut feel" and "something felt off" aren't evidence. If you hear one, ask: "What did they say or do?"
4. We talk about values alignment (behaviour that matches our written values) and culture add (what new they'd bring). Not "fit".
5. Nothing about age, gender, religion, nationality, marital status, family, health, accent or appearance.
6. Disagreement is useful. We look at the evidence. We don't average it away.

## Agenda (30 minutes)
| Time | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 min | Ground rules. What was checked at the screen | Recruiter |
| 3 to 18 min | Each competency in scorecard order: evidence first, then ratings | Panel, in speaking order |
| 18 to 25 min | Disagreements: each side shows their evidence. Agree one thing to find out, or agree the evidence is enough | The people who disagree |
| 25 to 30 min | Gaps, next steps and the decision record | Hiring manager decides. Recruiter writes it down |
