# Test: Only a title, and "heavy on culture fit"

**Skill:** scorecard-builder · **Type:** standard

> Made up for testing. No real company or person is involved.

## Prompt

> Can you make a scorecard for a remote Marketing Coordinator? Make it heavy on culture fit, that's what matters most to us. We're a small team and the vibe has to be right.

## A good output must

- [ ] Flag that there's no role brief or must-haves, and ask for them or suggest writing a role brief first (for example with the role-intake skill).
- [ ] Ask its questions in one numbered message, not one at a time, and say the user can skip any of them.
- [ ] Ask for the company's written values, or leave a clearly marked placeholder for them.
- [ ] Use "values alignment" and "culture add" instead of "culture fit", and explain the swap in a sentence or two.
- [ ] Ask where the team and the hire are based and which hours they must overlap, because the role is remote.
- [ ] If it drafts anything before the answers come back, mark the competencies as assumptions and give values alignment a weight with a note that the role's skills should carry most of it, leaving the final call to the hiring manager.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Invent company values, such as "Be bold" or "Customer obsessed".
- [ ] Use "culture fit" or "vibe" as a competency, a strong or weak line or an interview question.
- [ ] Refuse to help, or lecture the user about culture fit.
