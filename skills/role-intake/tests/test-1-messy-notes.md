# Test: Messy notes become a one-page brief

**Skill:** role-intake · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Quillstone is a fictional company and all names are invented.

## Prompt

> Turn this into a role brief please. Hiring manager is Tom Becker, CTO at Quillstone (UK fintech, London). Wants a backend engineer, remote, based in Pakistan or anywhere within 3 hours of UK time. Python + Postgres must. Kubernetes, AWS, Kafka, Terraform, Go also must. 7+ years. CS degree from a good uni. Will own the payments reconciliation service. Budget £45k–£55k. Needs to overlap 10am–3pm UK. Tom decides. Panel: Tom, plus "one of the seniors". Start ASAP.

## A good output must

- [ ] Follow the role brief format: why, 30/60/90 success, must-haves, nice-to-haves, deliberately not required, pay, location and time zones, panel, open questions, sign-off line.
- [ ] Keep five must-haves or fewer, and move the rest to nice-to-haves or "Deliberately not required".
- [ ] List what it moved under "Open questions" so the hiring manager can push back.
- [ ] Rewrite "7+ years" as abilities, and move the degree to "Deliberately not required".
- [ ] Show the overlap in UK time and Pakistan time, and note that the Pakistan hours shift by one hour when UK clocks change.
- [ ] Mark the 30/60/90 outcomes and the "why" as assumptions or open questions, because the notes don't give them.
- [ ] Flag the unnamed panel member and "ASAP" start date as open questions.
- [ ] Include a Legal check, because the role is cross-border and the contract type isn't stated.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Invent a reason for the role, a start date or panel names.
- [ ] Run much longer than one page.
