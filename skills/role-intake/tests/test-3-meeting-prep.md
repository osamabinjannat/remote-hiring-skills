# Test: Preparing for an intake meeting

**Skill:** role-intake · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up.

## Prompt

> I have an intake call tomorrow with a hiring manager who wants a remote QA engineer. I've never run one before. What should I ask?

## A good output must

- [ ] Give the intake questions as a numbered agenda, grouped by topic.
- [ ] Cover why the role exists, 30/60/90-day outcomes, must-haves, what can be learned, pay and currency, location, timezone overlap, the panel, the decision maker and the start date.
- [ ] Explain any HR term it uses, such as "must-have" or "overlap hours".
- [ ] Suggest booking interview slots during the call.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Write a role brief with invented answers.
- [ ] Ask the user the questions one at a time.
