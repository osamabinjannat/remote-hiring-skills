# Rejection emails

> Three blank emails to fill in by hand, one for each stage. Replace every [placeholder] and delete any line you don't need. The candidate-emails skill writes emails in this same format.

<!-- A person decides who gets these emails. These templates only help you say it kindly.
Never write "we went with a stronger candidate", "not a culture fit", "overqualified" or anything about age, gender, family, religion, nationality or health.
Only promise to keep someone's details if you really will. Then say for how long, and that they can ask you to delete them. -->

## After an application

**From:** [Your name], [Your role] · **To:** [Candidate's full name]

**Subject:** Your application for [Role] at [Company]

Hi [First name],

Thank you for applying for the [Role] role at [Company]. I'm sorry to tell you that we won't be taking your application further.

[Only if true: We'd like to keep your details for [length of time] in case a similar role opens. You can ask us to delete them at any time.]

Thank you again for your interest, and good luck with your search.

Best wishes,
[Your first name]

> **Before you send:** A person must read and edit this draft. Check names, dates, facts and tone.

## After an interview or exercise

**From:** [Your name], [Your role] · **To:** [Candidate's full name]

**Subject:** Your application for [Role] at [Company]

Hi [First name],

Thank you for your time on the [stage name, such as "written exercise" or "call with Rachel"]. I'm sorry to tell you that we won't be moving you to the next stage.

[Only if someone will write it: If you'd like short feedback, just reply and we'll send it within [number] working days.]

[Only if true: We'd like to keep your details for [length of time] in case a similar role opens. You can ask us to delete them at any time.]

Thank you again, and good luck with your search.

Best wishes,
[Your first name]

## After the final stage

<!-- This one comes from the hiring manager personally, by email or after a call. -->

**From:** [Hiring manager's name], [Their role] · **To:** [Candidate's full name]

**Subject:** Your application for [Role] at [Company]

Hi [First name],

Thank you for the time you gave us across all [number] stages of our process. I'm sorry to tell you that we won't be offering you the [Role] role.

[Optional, and only the panel's own points: I'd like to share [two] points from the panel. [A genuine strength, with the stage it came from.] [One thing to work on, with the stage it came from.]]

If it would help to talk this through, I'd be glad to have a short call. I'm free on [day and date] at [time] [candidate's time zone] ([time] [team's time zone]). Just reply with what suits you.

Thank you again, and I wish you all the best for what comes next.

Warm wishes,
[Your first name]
