# Status update or delay

**Subject:** Update on your application for [Role]

[If you're late: I'm sorry we've kept you waiting. You should have heard from us by [original date], and that's on us.] [Optional: one honest sentence on why, such as "Our hiring manager was on leave and we didn't plan for it."]

You'll hear from us by [day and date], even if we haven't decided by then.

Thank you for your patience. If you have any questions in the meantime, just reply.

Best wishes,
[Your first name]
