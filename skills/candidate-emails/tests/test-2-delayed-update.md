# Test: An honest update after a long delay

**Skill:** candidate-emails · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Tallowmere is a fictional company and all names are invented.

## Prompt

> We messed up. Usman Tariq interviewed with our hiring manager Jo Whitfield three weeks ago for the Frontend Developer role and nobody has told him anything since. Jo was on holiday and we didn't cover for her. She's back now and says she'll decide by Thursday 19 November. Usman's in Islamabad, we're in London. I'm Sam Ellis, the recruiter at Tallowmere. Can you write him something?

## A good output must

- [ ] Say sorry plainly in the first two sentences, and own the delay as the company's.
- [ ] Give the honest reason in one short sentence, such as the hiring manager being on leave, without blaming Jo.
- [ ] Give the new date in full, "Thursday 19 November", and promise an update by then even if there's no decision yet.
- [ ] Come from Sam, stay under 150 words and sound like a person, not a policy.
- [ ] Suggest in "Notes on this draft" that Sam checks the date with Jo, or sends another update on the 19th if there's still no decision.
- [ ] End with the "Before you send" footer, word for word.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Hint at the outcome, for example "you're a strong candidate" or "don't worry".
- [ ] Invent progress, such as "we're checking references".
- [ ] Use corporate filler such as "due to unforeseen circumstances" or "we regret any inconvenience".
