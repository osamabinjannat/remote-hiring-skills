# Test: An offer through an employer of record

**Skill:** candidate-emails · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Quillstone is a fictional company and all names are invented.

## Prompt

> Need the offer email for Hira Siddiqui. I offered her the Senior Data Analyst job on the phone yesterday. She's in Karachi. She'll be employed through our employer of record, not by Quillstone directly. £3,200 a month gross. Start Monday 4 January 2027. 25 days holiday plus public holidays. Core hours 10am to 3pm UK time. Add the usual bits about probation and notice period too. I'm Tom Becker, CTO at Quillstone in London.

## A good output must

- [ ] Confirm the offer in the first two sentences, and list only the terms Tom gave: £3,200 a month gross, employed through the employer of record, start Monday 4 January 2027, 25 days holiday plus public holidays.
- [ ] Show core hours as 3pm to 8pm Pakistan time (10am to 3pm UK time), and say they move to 2pm to 7pm Pakistan time when UK clocks go forward in late March.
- [ ] Explain "employer of record" in brackets the first time it appears.
- [ ] Use [placeholders] for the probation period and notice period, or leave them to the contract, and ask Tom for the details in "Notes on this draft".
- [ ] Use a [placeholder] for the reply-by date, because Tom didn't give one, and list it in the notes.
- [ ] Include a Legal check covering employment through an employer of record, cross-border pay (a gross figure in pounds for someone paid in Pakistan) and notice periods.
- [ ] End with the "Before you send" footer, word for word.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Invent a probation length, notice period, exchange rate or take-home pay figure.
- [ ] Add legal wording such as "at-will", "subject to satisfactory references" or a non-compete.
- [ ] Describe Hira as a contractor.
