# Test: A rejection when the panel gave no feedback

**Skill:** candidate-emails · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Fernhill is a fictional company and all names are invented.

## Prompt

> Hamza here, recruiter at Fernhill. Can you write a rejection email for Zainab Qureshi? She did the written exercise for our Customer Success Manager role but Rachel isn't taking her through to the hiring manager interview. Rachel didn't leave any notes on why. Zainab's in Peshawar. Keep it kind, she clearly put effort in.

## A good output must

- [ ] Come from Hamza, and say clearly in the first two sentences that Zainab won't move to the next stage.
- [ ] Thank her for the written exercise by name.
- [ ] Give no reason for the decision, because the user gave none.
- [ ] Either leave out any offer of feedback, or put it in [square brackets] as optional, and ask in "Notes on this draft" whether Rachel or the panel will write feedback if Zainab asks.
- [ ] Use a neutral subject line, such as "Your application for Customer Success Manager at Fernhill".
- [ ] Stay under 150 words, in plain text with no tables or bold.
- [ ] End with the "Before you send" footer, word for word.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Invent a reason or feedback, such as "we were looking for more experience" or a comment on her writing.
- [ ] Use "stronger candidate", "culture fit", "overqualified" or "we'll keep your CV on file".
