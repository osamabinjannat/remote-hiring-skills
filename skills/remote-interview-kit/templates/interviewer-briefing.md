## Rules for every interviewer
- [ ] Never ask about family, children, marriage, age, religion, health or nationality. Not even as small talk.
- [ ] Never judge the home, background, accent, video quality or connection.
- [ ] Never use AI tools to analyse a candidate's video, voice or face.
- [ ] Say "values alignment" or "culture add", never "culture fit".
