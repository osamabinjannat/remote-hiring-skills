# Test: A long loop with an unpaid 8-hour take-home

**Skill:** remote-interview-kit · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Kestrel Pay is a fictional company and all names are invented.

## Prompt

> Can you turn our interview process into a proper interview plan with questions? We're Kestrel Pay, a 40-person fintech in London, hiring a remote senior backend engineer based in Pakistan. Interviews run from 19 October to 6 November. Here's what we do now:
>
> 1. Recruiter call, 30 min
> 2. Online coding test, 45 min
> 3. Take-home: build a small payments API. About 8 hours. We don't pay for it, everyone does them
> 4. Technical interview, 1 hour
> 5. System design, 45 min
> 6. Pair programming with two engineers, 1 hour
> 7. Culture fit chat with both founders, 30 min
>
> It's about 10 hours all in. Team works 9:30 to 5:30 UK time. Scorecard: Python and API design 30%, system design 20%, code quality and testing 20%, written async communication 15%, values alignment 15%. Panel: me (Aisha, recruiter, London), Tom (CTO), Wei and Grace (senior engineers), and the founders, Ben and Leila.

## A good output must

- [ ] Add up the current loop correctly as 12 hours 30 minutes, not "about 10 hours", and say it's far over the 4-hour limit.
- [ ] Trim the loop to under 4 hours of candidate time, and show the new total with the right sum.
- [ ] Do one of these with the take-home: cut it to 60 minutes or less, pay for it at a stated rate (or "Not yet agreed") or replace it with a live exercise.
- [ ] Say in "Notes on this draft" what it cut or merged and why, one line each, for example merging the coding test into a shorter take-home.
- [ ] Keep every scorecard competency tested in at least one stage, with Python and API design (30%) tested in two.
- [ ] Rename the "culture fit chat" as a values conversation about values alignment and culture add, and ask for Kestrel Pay's written values instead of inventing them.
- [ ] Show every time in UK and Pakistan time, and note that UK clocks go back on Sunday 25 October 2026. For example, 9:30am UK is 1:30pm in Pakistan before the change and 2:30pm after it.
- [ ] Give one stage guide per stage, with an opening, questions mapped to competencies, follow-up probes, what to listen for and a closing.
- [ ] Include a Legal check if it suggests paying candidates for the take-home across borders.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Keep any unpaid take-home over 60 minutes.
- [ ] Use "culture fit", except to say it was renamed.
- [ ] Show any interview time in only one time zone.
