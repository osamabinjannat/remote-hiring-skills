# Test: The company won't publish the salary

**Skill:** job-description-writer · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Larchmont Books and all names are fictional.

## Prompt

> Need a job post for a remote bookkeeper. We don't publish salaries, just write it without. (Budget is CAD 1,500 to 2,000 a month as a contractor, but that's internal only.)
>
> About us: Larchmont Books is a 12-person bookkeeping firm in Toronto. We keep the books for about 150 restaurants and cafes in Ontario.
>
> The role: based in Pakistan, contractor, full time. Keeps the books for about 30 restaurant clients in QuickBooks Online. Must be able to reconcile bank and card accounts, run month-end close, chase missing receipts by email in clear English and catch errors before they reach our accountant. Xero is a bonus. Overlap 9am to 12pm Toronto time, Monday to Friday.
>
> Hiring: 20-minute call with me, a paid 90-minute bookkeeping test, then a 45-minute call with our partner Grace Wei. We reply to everyone within a week.
>
> Benefits: 15 days paid time off, plus QuickBooks certification paid for.
>
> Apply through the careers page on our website. Thanks! Sana

## A good output must

- [ ] Draft the full post in the same reply, without refusing or holding it back.
- [ ] Put "[Pay range: add before posting]" (or very close wording) in the key facts box.
- [ ] Explain once, in one line, why pay matters: posts with pay get more applicants, and some places legally require a pay range in job posts.
- [ ] Include a Legal check on pay transparency rules, using the standard wording.
- [ ] Show the overlap in Toronto and Pakistan time: 6pm to 9pm in Pakistan while Toronto is on daylight saving time (second Sunday in March to first Sunday in November), and 7pm to 10pm the rest of the year.
- [ ] Show "Contractor, full time" and "based in Pakistan" in the key facts box.
- [ ] Keep five must-haves or fewer and put Xero under "Nice to have" with the "If you're close, apply" line.
- [ ] List the pay placeholder in Notes.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Put the CAD 1,500 to 2,000 budget, or any other pay figure, in the post.
- [ ] Refuse, lecture or make the pay point more than once.
- [ ] Invent benefits or company facts that Sana didn't give.
