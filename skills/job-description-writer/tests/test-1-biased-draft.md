# Test: A biased wish-list draft with no salary

**Skill:** job-description-writer · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Tidewell is a fictional company.

## Prompt

> Our CTO wrote this JD and wants it on LinkedIn today. Can you clean it up without changing what we're looking for?
>
> **Frontend Rockstar Ninja (React)**
>
> Tidewell is a young, dynamic team in Dubai building shipment tracking software for freight forwarders. We're fast-paced and we work hard and play hard. We need a rockstar ninja who can hit the ground running. He will own our customer dashboard and work closely with our two designers.
>
> **Requirements**
> - 10+ years of frontend experience
> - BS in Computer Science from a reputable university
> - Native English speaker
> - Expert in React
> - TypeScript
> - Next.js
> - Redux
> - GraphQL
> - Jest and Cypress testing
> - Figma
> - CI/CD pipelines
> - AWS
> - Must be a culture fit
> - Young and energetic
>
> Remote, Pakistan. Gulf hours. Salary: competitive.

## A good output must

- [ ] Use a plain, searchable title such as "Frontend Developer (React, Remote, Pakistan)", with no "rockstar" or "ninja".
- [ ] Ask for the pay range (currency, per month or year, gross or net, salary or contractor fee), and put "[Pay range: add before posting]" in the key facts box instead of "competitive".
- [ ] Cut the 14 requirements to five must-haves or fewer, each written as something the person can do, plus three nice-to-haves or fewer followed by "You don't need all of these. If you're close, apply."
- [ ] Say in Notes which requirements it moved to nice-to-haves and which it cut.
- [ ] Rewrite "10+ years" as abilities and drop the degree requirement.
- [ ] Replace "Native English speaker" with "fluent written and spoken English" or close to it.
- [ ] Log every swapped phrase in the inclusion check table (Original wording, Changed to, Why), including "rockstar ninja", "young, dynamic", "young and energetic", "culture fit", "work hard and play hard", "fast-paced" and "He will".
- [ ] Ask for the exact overlap hours, because "Gulf hours" is vague, or show them in UAE and Pakistan time and say neither country changes its clocks.
- [ ] Use placeholders for the missing hiring stages, benefits and how to apply, and list each one in Notes.
- [ ] Show the word count and stay under 700 words.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Keep "rockstar", "ninja", "native English speaker", "culture fit" or "he" or "she" for the new hire anywhere in the post.
- [ ] List more than five must-haves or more than three nice-to-haves.
- [ ] Invent a salary, benefits, interview stages or company facts.
