# Test: Turning a London office job into a remote post

**Skill:** job-description-writer · **Type:** standard

> Everything here is made up. Quayside Homeware and all names are fictional.

## Prompt

> Can you turn our current ad into a remote one? Same job, but we want to hire someone in Pakistan to work with the London team. Budget for the remote version is GBP 1,100 to 1,400 a month as a contractor. They'd need to overlap 9am to 1pm UK time.
>
> **Marketing Coordinator, London (office-based)**
>
> Quayside Homeware is a 40-person homeware brand working from our Shoreditch studio.
>
> - Office-based 5 days a week in Shoreditch
> - Salary GBP 30,000 to 34,000 plus 5% pension
> - Private health insurance, cycle-to-work scheme, free breakfast Fridays
> - 25 days holiday plus bank holidays
> - You'll schedule our Instagram posts and email newsletters, write product descriptions, work with photographers on shoots and report weekly on campaign results
> - Must have: 3+ years in marketing, a marketing degree, excellent communication, Canva, Klaviyo, Shopify, Google Analytics
> - Interviews: 2 rounds in person at our studio
>
> Apply with your CV and a cover letter to Olivia Grant, Head of Marketing.

## A good output must

- [ ] Use a plain title such as "Marketing Coordinator (Remote, Pakistan)".
- [ ] Put the remote pay in the key facts box as GBP 1,100 to 1,400 a month, contractor fee, with gross or net shown as a placeholder or asked about.
- [ ] Show the overlap as 9am to 1pm UK time, which is 1pm to 5pm in Pakistan while UK clocks are on British Summer Time (last Sunday in March to last Sunday in October) and 2pm to 6pm the rest of the year, and say the Pakistan hours shift by an hour when UK clocks change.
- [ ] Remove office-only details: the Shoreditch studio as a workplace, 5 days in the office, the cycle-to-work scheme and free breakfast.
- [ ] Leave out the UK employee benefits (pension, private health insurance, 25 days plus bank holidays) unless confirmed for a contractor in Pakistan, and ask about them or use placeholders listed in Notes.
- [ ] Ask about or use a placeholder for the interview stages, because "2 rounds in person at our studio" won't work for a remote hire.
- [ ] Flag "work with photographers on shoots" as a question in Notes, because it may need someone in person.
- [ ] Rewrite "3+ years" as abilities, drop the marketing degree and keep five must-haves or fewer.
- [ ] Include a Legal check on hiring a contractor in another country.

## A good output must not

- [ ] Show the GBP 30,000 to 34,000 office salary, or convert the pay into another currency.
- [ ] Mention commuting, office days or in-person interviews as part of the remote role.
- [ ] Invent remote benefits, such as an equipment or internet allowance, that the user didn't give.
